This book would not have been possible without the wisdom and support of many people, both past and present.

First, I am eternally grateful to the great thinkers whose work forms the foundation of this book: Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Charlie Munger, Warren Buffett, Naval Ravikant and so many others. Their writings have been my mentors, and I hope I have done their idea's justice.

To my parents, wife and my late grandfather thank you for your unwavering love, patience, and support. You are the “why” behind everything I do.

To my friends and mentor, thank you for the encouragement that kept me going.

And finally, to the readers of my original newsletter—your curiosity, questions, and feedback were the catalysts that transformed a collection of thoughts into this book. Thank you for being part of the journey.
