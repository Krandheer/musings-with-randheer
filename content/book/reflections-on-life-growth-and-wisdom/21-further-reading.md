The ideas in this book stand on the shoulders of giants. For those who wish to continue their journey of learning, here is a curated list of the books and resources that have most profoundly shaped my thinking and this book.

## Part 1: Philosophy & Inner Wisdom
- **Meditations** by Marcus Aurelius.
- **Man's Search for Meaning** by Viktor Frankl.
- **Dialogues and letters** by Seneca.

## Part 2: Personal Development & Growth
- **Atomic Habits** by James Clear.
- **Mindset: The New Psychology of Success** by Carol S. Dweck.
- **Emotional Intelligent** by Daniel Goleman.

## Part 3: Financial Wisdom & Investment
- **The Intelligent Investor** by Benjamin Graham.
- **The Millionaire Next Door** by Thomas J. Stanley and William D.
- **Berkshire Hathaway Shareholder Letters** by Warren Buffett.

## Part 4: Mental Models and Decision-Making
- **100 mental models** by wisdom theory
- **The great mental models vol1 and vol2** by FS farman street

## Part 5: Mental Models & Decision-Making
- **Poor Charlie's Almanack** by Peter D. Kaufman — The essential collection of Charlie Munger's wit and wisdom.
- **The Almanack of Naval Ravikant** by Eric Jorgenson.

## Part 6: Life Lessons & Practical Wisdom
- **Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind** by Yuval Noah Harari.
- **The Laws of Human Nature** by Robert Greene.
